# FAQ

## The enchanting table shows an empty catalogue

You need to make enchantments available first. Find enchanted books in structure chests (see [[Book Locations]]) and place them in chiseled bookshelves within 2 blocks of the enchanting table. Each book permanently adds one enchantment to the catalogue.

## Not enough slots to apply an enchantment

Every item has a maximum slot count based on the material (see [[Enchanting Table]]). Each enchantment level costs 1 slot. You gain extra slots by applying curses (+1 per curse) or by upgrading to a better material tier.

## Where do I find Mending?

End Cities. Mending is the rarest book in the mod, gated behind the endgame. The cost is 3 slots and 8 XP levels.

## Where did Sharpness, Protection, Efficiency, and Unbreaking go?

Smithing upgrades replaced them: Honing (damage), Warding (protection), Grinding (mining speed), Tempering (durability). Apply them at the [[Smithing Table]] using templates found in structures.

## How do I remove enchantments?

Use the grindstone. It strips all enchantments from the item, curses included, and the item keeps all of its slots (see [[Anvil and Grindstone]]).

## Why does gold armor have more enchantment slots than diamond?

By design. Gold has 6 slots (the highest) but the lowest protection. Gold rewards players who sacrifice defense for magical options.

## Do mobs spawn with enchanted gear?

Yes. The chance scales with difficulty. On Hard mode, up to 60% of mobs spawn with one enchanted piece and up to 40% with a smithing upgrade. Disable the feature with `/gamerule enchantment-overhaul:mob_gear_enchantments false`. See [[Mob Equipment]] for the full breakdown.

## What are the visual overlays on armor?

Warding and Tempering upgrades applied at the [[Smithing Table]] show as visible overlays on the 3D armor model, tinted to match the armor material. Both stack and are compatible with vanilla cosmetic trims.

## Does the mod work with other enchanting mods?

The mod completely replaces the vanilla enchanting table UI. Mods adding enchantments to the vanilla system will be suppressed unless they are registered through the catalogue. Mods adding new items or armor materials need their slot counts configured separately.

## Chiseled bookshelves vs. normal bookshelves?

Chiseled bookshelves hold enchanted books and add enchantments to the catalogue. Normal bookshelves reduce the reagent cost of enchanting (up to 50% with 15 bookshelves). Both emit particles toward the enchanting table when active.
