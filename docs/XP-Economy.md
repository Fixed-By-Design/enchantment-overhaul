# XP Economy

XP is consumed by three systems. Mending still converts XP orbs into durability, competing with all other XP sinks.

| System | XP Range | Details |
|---|---|---|
| [[Enchanting Table]] | 2-10 levels | 2 (level I), 4 (II), 7 (III), 10 (IV+). Mending: 8. |
| [[Anvil and Grindstone]] (repair) | 1 level | Flat cost per repair |
| Mending | Passive | XP orbs repair equipped items instead of filling the XP bar |

The [[Smithing Table]] no longer costs XP. The only cost is the template and the material.

## Tension

Every XP level spent on enchanting is a level Mending cannot use for repairs. Players who enchant aggressively will find their gear degrading faster. This creates a natural pacing mechanism.
